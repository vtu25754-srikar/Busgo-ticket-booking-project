================================================================================
BUSGO TRAVEL KNOWLEDGE BASE: PASSENGER TRAVEL GUIDELINES
================================================================================

1. REPORTING AND BOARDING TIME
- Passengers must report at their chosen boarding point at least 15 to 20 minutes prior to scheduled departure.
- Live bus GPS tracking links are sent via SMS 60 minutes before departure to help passengers monitor bus proximity in real time.
- Buses cannot wait beyond scheduled departure times due to strict highway transport and permit schedules.

2. MANDATORY PHOTO IDENTIFICATION
All adult passengers must carry a valid government-issued photo ID during travel, such as:
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Voter ID Card
- Driving License
- PAN Card (supplemented with address verification if requested)

3. ONBOARD CONDUCT AND SAFETY
- Smoking and consumption of alcoholic beverages are strictly prohibited inside the bus and boarding premises. Violators will be deboarded immediately without refund.
- Passengers should use earphones when playing music, audiobooks, or videos on mobile devices between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM to respect sleeping co-passengers.

4. PET POLICY
For hygiene, passenger safety, and allergy considerations, live pets (including dogs, cats, and birds) are NOT permitted inside passenger air-conditioned sleeper cabins or seating areas.

5. AMENITIES AND COMPLIMENTARY SERVICES
Most premium operators (Orange Travels, VRL, IntrCity SmartBus, Zingbus) provide:
- Individual USB charging points and 220V plug sockets
- Sanitized blankets and pillows on AC Sleeper routes
- 500ml complimentary mineral water bottle
- Central air conditioning and reading lights
