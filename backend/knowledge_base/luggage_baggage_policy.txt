================================================================================
BUSGO TRAVEL KNOWLEDGE BASE: LUGGAGE & BAGGAGE GUIDELINES
================================================================================

1. COMPLIMENTARY BAGGAGE ALLOWANCE
Each paying passenger is permitted to carry up to 15 kilograms (kg) of personal baggage without any additional charge. This typically includes:
- One medium-sized suitcase or duffle bag to be stored in the bus luggage boot (under carriage).
- One small personal backpack, handbag, or laptop bag to be kept inside the passenger cabin.

2. EXCESS BAGGAGE CHARGES
Any luggage exceeding the 15 kg limit is classified as excess baggage.
- Charges for excess baggage vary between ₹20 to ₹50 per additional kilogram, payable directly to the bus conductor/operator at the time of boarding.
- Extra-large cargo items, commercial consignments, or bulk freight must be booked in advance as parcel cargo and cannot be placed in the standard passenger luggage hold.

3. PROHIBITED AND DANGEROUS GOODS
For the safety of all travellers, the following items are strictly prohibited on all BUSGO partner buses:
- Flammable materials (gas cylinders, kerosene, petrol, fireworks, lighter fluid).
- Explosives, ammunition, and firearms.
- Corrosive acids, toxic chemicals, radioactive materials.
- Contraband, narcotics, illicit substances.
- Perishable and pungent foodstuffs that cause offensive odors inside closed air-conditioned cabins.

4. VALUABLES AND ELECTRONICS LIABILITY
Passengers are advised to keep high-value items—such as cash, jewelry, laptops, cameras, passports, and essential medications—in their personal carry-on bag at all times. Neither BUSGO nor the transport operators assume liability for loss or damage to unchecked valuables placed in the bus luggage compartment.
