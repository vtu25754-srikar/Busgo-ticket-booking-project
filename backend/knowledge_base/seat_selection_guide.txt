================================================================================
BUSGO TRAVEL KNOWLEDGE BASE: SEAT SELECTION & DECK GUIDE
================================================================================

1. LOWER DECK VS UPPER DECK IN MULTI-AXLE SLEEPER BUSES
- Lower Deck:
  * Offers lower center of gravity resulting in minimal bus sway, gentler ride quality, and reduced motion sickness.
  * Easy boarding and disembarkation without climbing stairs or ladders, making it strongly recommended for senior citizens, pregnant women, and travelers with physical mobility constraints.
- Upper Deck:
  * Offers greater personal privacy, elevated scenic road views, and less cabin foot-traffic disturbance.
  * Ideal for younger travelers and solo backpackers seeking quiet rest.

2. SEAT AND BERTH CONFIGURATIONS
- Single Sleeper Berths (Window Side, 1+2 layout): Completely private individual berths along the left side of the bus. High demand for solo travelers.
- Double Sleeper Berths (2+1 layout): Two adjacent berths on the right side. Perfect for couples, families, and friends travelling together.
- Seater Buses (2+2 layout): Ergonomic push-back reclining seats equipped with individual footrests and reading lights.

3. EXCLUSIVE LADIES SEAT RESERVATION POLICY
- Seats and berths designated with a female icon or highlighted in pink are reserved exclusively for solo female travelers.
- A male passenger cannot book a berth immediately adjacent to an already booked female passenger unless they are booking together as part of the same transaction (e.g. family or couple).
- If an unreserved ladies seat remains vacant within 2 hours of departure, operators may release it for general booking.
